## Battery automation

Automatic battery hardware writes remain disabled. Main includes stale-source freshness checks and reversible opt-in policy. PR #118 is merged into main and included in this source merge; its post-event sample refresh barriers passed CI. Real AC/Battery transition and suspend/resume behavior has not been observed. Keep #68 and #87 open until that evidence exists.

## Required evidence before enabling or advertising writes

1. Supervised per-model fan Apply/Auto/rollback/restart validation, including temperature response and helper interruption.
2. Supervised Aura effect validation on exact allow-listed hardware.
3. PolicyKit authorization, denial/cancellation, missing-agent, and backend-loss behavior in the target session.
4. Documented machine/distro/backend record and independent review of evidence.

Until then, retain fail-closed gates and label those controls unverified or unavailable.
