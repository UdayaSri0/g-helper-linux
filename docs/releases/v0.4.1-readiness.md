## Security status

- The audit reported no vulnerability. Three unmaintained-dependency warnings remain: `zbus_names 4.3.1` (RUSTSEC-2026-0371), `zvariant 5.9.2` (RUSTSEC-2026-0370), and `zvariant_derive 5.9.2` (RUSTSEC-2026-0372). Preserve these warnings; do not suppress them.
- Unsigned executable replacement remains disabled. Automatic battery hardware writes remain disabled. Fan control remains fail-closed unless exact hardware capability and safety validation pass; generic PWM and uncertain channels stay read-only.
- No privileged helper, PolicyKit, D-Bus, or udev permission boundary was loosened by the release changes.

## Publication boundary

`.github/workflows/release.yml` triggers on pushes to tags matching `v*`, and also permits manual dispatch. Build jobs require the tag name to match the workspace version (`v0.4.1`). After the Linux and Fedora build jobs succeed, `publish-release` receives the workflow's only `contents: write` permission and calls `softprops/action-gh-release` to create/publish the GitHub Release and upload the matched assets. It is not configured as a draft release.

Therefore **pushing `v0.4.1` is the automatic publication boundary**. Dispatching the workflow on a version tag also enters the publisher path. Do not push that tag or dispatch on it until the final human publication approval is explicit. No tag, release, or production asset upload has been created in this preparation.
